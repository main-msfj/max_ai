# AI Capabilities Report: Tools, Skills, and Context (MCP)

This report outlines the concepts of how AI systems leverage external tools, internal skills, and contextual information, defined here as M.C.P.

## 1. The Role of Tools (External Capabilities)
Tools represent the external capabilities an AI can invoke to perform actions beyond its core language understanding.

*   **Function:** Tools allow the AI to interface with external systems (e.g., calculators, APIs, databases) to gather real-time data or execute complex computations.
*   **Mechanism:** An AI uses its reasoning to determine *when* and *how* to call a specific tool based on the user's request.
*   **Benefit:** Enhances the AI's utility by allowing it to move from simple information recall to actionable execution.

## 2. The Role of Skills (Internal Knowledge)
Skills represent the specialized, learned knowledge or procedural abilities the AI possesses.

*   **Function:** Skills are the internal knowledge base and learned procedures that allow the AI to understand complex concepts, follow logical steps, and perform specific tasks (e.g., code generation, summarization, logical deduction).
*   **Mechanism:** Skills are often represented as complex internal algorithms or specialized modules.
*   **Benefit:** Provides the AI with the necessary intelligence to process information and structure responses coherently.

## 3. The Role of Context (Contextual Awareness)
Context is the surrounding information that frames the request and dictates the appropriate application of tools and skills.

*   **Function:** Context provides the necessary background, constraints, and goals for the task. It ensures the AI’s output is relevant, accurate, and tailored to the user's specific situation.
*   **Mechanism:** Context is derived from the prompt, previous conversation history, and external data provided by the user or the environment.
*   **Benefit:** Bridges the gap between raw capability (skills/tools) and useful output (relevant answers).

## Conclusion: The Synergy
The true power of advanced AI lies in the synergy between these three elements:

*   **Context** determines **what** needs to be done.
*   **Skills** provide the **how** (the internal method).
*   **Tools** provide the **what** (the external action).

By integrating context, skills, and tools, an AI can move beyond simple prompting to become a powerful, adaptive problem-solver.
