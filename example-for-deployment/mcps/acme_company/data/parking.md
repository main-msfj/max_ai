# Garage tips

- Always look both ways before crossing the ramp; cars come up from B2.
- Drive slowly (10 km/h) and keep your headlights on inside the garage.
- Park in reverse so you can leave facing forward.
- Maximum vehicle height is 2.1 m.
- Lock your car and take your valuables with you.
- Report scratches or incidents to the security desk on B1.
- Remember your spot: write it down (for example "B1-03") when you park.
