# Before you go on vacation

- Set an out-of-office reply in your email with your return date and a backup contact.
- Update your chat status and add the dates to the team calendar.
- Hand over anything urgent: write down open tasks and who takes each one.
- If you are on call, swap your shifts before you leave.
- Save your work, push your branches, and shut down your laptop.
- On your first day back, check the handover notes before your inbox.
